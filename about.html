About QZIX & Its Founder

Why QZIX COIN was created
Since the day I was born, I have watched the prices of almost everything rise over time. As I grew up, I realised that things which once seemed affordable often became more expensive, while seeing prices come down seemed like only a dream. This thought inspired me to create QZIX COIN. My idea is to build a digital token project with only 100 fixed tokens, where the goal is to create a system designed around long-term value growth, a fixed supply and transparent rules. I want QZIX COIN to represent the hope that its value may grow over time rather than simply creating more tokens whenever demand increases.

The founder's vision
To build a simple, organised and transparent digital-token system with a fixed supply of 100 tokens. Every token has a unique ID, and ownership is recorded after payment is verified and approved. This is the project's vision, not a guarantee: the token price may rise or fall, and no future price or profit is guaranteed.

Project principles
Transparency, clear rules and careful payment verification. A token is assigned to a buyer only after the administrator verifies the actual payment. The project does not promise profit or future price growth.
